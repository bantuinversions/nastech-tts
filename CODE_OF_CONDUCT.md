# Nastech Research Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Nastech Research is committed to a welcoming, professional, and evidence-led collaboration space. Contributors, maintainers, users, and reviewers are expected to participate in good faith and to respect the safety, privacy, and dignity of others.

## Expected conduct

Constructive technical disagreement is welcome when it is specific, respectful, and grounded in reproducible evidence. Contributors should protect credentials, private recordings, consent records, personal data, and any unpublished security information. Claims about voices, models, quality, platform support, or benchmarks must be clearly scoped and reproducible.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate disclosure of private or sensitive material, misuse of voice technology, plagiarism, and knowingly misleading product claims are not acceptable. Do not upload voices without the necessary rights and consent, and do not use public issues for vulnerability disclosure.

## Enforcement

Project stewards may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent participation restriction. To report a concern, follow the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or contact the repository maintainers through the private channel referenced there.

## Scope

This code applies to repository spaces, release discussions, issue and pull-request interactions, and any event or communication explicitly representing Nastech Research or Nastech TTS.
