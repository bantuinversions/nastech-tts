## Nastech Voice Core

Nastech Voice Core is the local synthesis component used by Nastech TTS. Deployments use the Nastech TTS local runtime, retain audio on the operator’s device by default, and do not proxy synthesis through a cloud service.

## Voice and Training Notice

Any custom voice-style asset or future fine-tune must use recordings supplied or licensed by the operator with documented speaker consent. Nastech TTS must not be used to impersonate a person without authorization.
