## Nastech TTS v0.12.0 — Lazy Bantu model packs

Nastech TTS v0.12.0 formalizes the **local-first, on-demand Bantu model-pack architecture** under the Nastech Research brand.

### Highlights

- Optional Bantu MMS-TTS packs are external assets and are never downloaded or loaded during startup.
- REST, CLI, and agent-callable operations expose pack inventory and explicit single-language downloads.
- The `mms-lazy` runtime loads the requested local model and evicts the previous model, keeping one optional MMS model resident at a time.
- CPU/GPU detection and memory-aware execution are shared with the core runtime; unavailable CUDA fails closed rather than pretending to accelerate.
- Verified public routes cover Luganda, Runyankole, Acholi, Ateso, Kiswahili, Kinyarwanda, Kirundi, Gikuyu, Sepedi, Tshivenda, Xitsonga, Shona, and Chichewa/Nyanja.
- Languages without a verified checkpoint remain explicit `no-verified-pack` entries. Nastech does not silently substitute another language.
- The compact core remains under the 1 GiB deployment budget and performs real local inference without cloud proxies.

### Verification

The release was validated with **135 deterministic tests**, generated REST/agent contracts, package metadata checks, wheel and source-distribution checks, and a compact-budget measurement of approximately **699.23 MiB** on the release host. The included Bantu fixtures are local CPU-generated WAV smoke outputs; they are not native-speaker or production-quality certification.

### Licensing and assets

The compact source distribution remains Apache-2.0. MMS model packs are optional external assets governed by their upstream licenses, including CC-BY-NC-4.0 where applicable; no optional model pack is bundled in the wheel or source distribution.
